About

Schuyler Hample
(SKY-ler)

Charleston, SC Fort Lauderdale, FL Miami, FL

Schuyler Hample — aka Schuy — is a digital artist and designer. The work lives where the cinematic meets the synthetic: deep color, geometric tension, and light that feels both engineered and alive.

Print, stage visual, or design system — the goal is the same: immersive digital imagery at the highest possible pixel count.

Available for select collaborations, commissions, and creative direction. Websites and brand identity for businesses run through Hample Design House.
