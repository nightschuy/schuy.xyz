How it works

From noise to roadmap in three steps

Stop pasting screenshots into docs. Driftline does the collecting, clustering, and prioritizing for you.

Capture everything

One-click integrations pull in feedback from support tickets, sales calls, app reviews, and Slack — automatically, in real time.

Cluster with AI

Driftline groups thousands of raw comments into clean themes, tags sentiment, and links each theme to revenue at stake.

Rank by impact

Every theme is scored by reach, revenue, and effort — so the next sprint plans itself and stakeholders stop guessing.
